**ORBIT BREAKER** is a tiny vertical-arena survival shooter built by an AI agent.
The video compares the **exact same 15 seconds of gameplay** on **Day 1** (the first working prototype) versus **Day 7** (after a week of daily AI updates):

- **Same map:** Identical bounded arena (`x: 110..970, y: 430..1530` in 1080×1920 space) with the exact same 4 symmetric tactical pillars.
